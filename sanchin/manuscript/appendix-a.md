This appendix gathers the essentials of the kata onto a few pages for quick reference in the dojo or at home. Photocopy it, or keep the book open beside you while you practise.

## The Ground-Up Posture Checklist

| Area | Check |
|---|---|
| Feet | Shoulder width; front heel level with rear toes; front toes in 20–30°; rear foot forward or slightly in |
| Contact | Heel, ball of big toe, ball of little toe; toes long and pressing, not curled |
| Knees | Slight bend; kneecaps over second or third toes; thighs pressing gently in |
| Pelvis | Slightly tucked; buttocks, lower abdomen and pelvic floor gently engaged; hips level |
| Ribs | Lowest ribs settled down and in |
| Shoulders | Blades drawn down into the back; tops soft |
| Arms (block) | Forearm upright; fist at shoulder height, in line with outer shoulder; elbow a fist from the body |
| Head | Chin slightly back; neck long; crown lifting |
| Eyes and face | Level gaze at a fixed point; calm face; jaw relaxed |
| Breath | In through nose, low and all round; out through mouth, steady, throat never closed |
| Tension | Baseline 3–4 in legs, hips, back; peak 5–8 at end of technique; face and neck 1–2 |

: **Table A.2** The posture checklist from Chapter 6, with breath and tension added.

## The Punch in Five Points

1. Fist at the side of the body, palm up, elbow straight back.
2. Straight path to the centre line at solar-plexus height, elbow brushing the ribs.
3. Rotation in the last third, arriving palm down.
4. Rear foot presses, hips square, back delivers the arm, shoulder stays down.
5. Out-breath ends as the punch arrives; elbow not locked; wrist straight.

## The Turn in Five Points

1. Settle completely before you begin.
2. Rear foot slides across, behind the front heel, to shoulder-width beyond it.
3. Pivot on both feet through 180°, head level, eyes arriving with the body.
4. Arrive in a correct stance without adjustment.
5. Breath and baseline tension continuous throughout.

## Safety in Five Points

1. Never hold the breath; never close the throat completely.
2. Stop at once for dizziness, sudden headache or sharp joint pain.
3. Knees always over the toes.
4. Testing only with consent, under supervision, building gradually; never the head, neck, spine, kidneys, groin, joints or floating ribs.
5. With high blood pressure, heart or eye conditions, pregnancy or hernia, seek medical advice first and use gentle breathing.
