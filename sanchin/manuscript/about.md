# About the Author

Dr Beverly J. Stivers is a teacher and long-time practitioner of Okinawan Goju-Ryu karate. Over many years of training and teaching, Sanchin has remained at the centre of Dr Stivers's practice, and the work has involved students of every age and level, from children taking their first class to senior black belts and people returning to karate later in life.

Dr Stivers's teaching is known for its clarity and its problem-solving approach: finding the real cause of a fault rather than repeating the same correction, and giving students practical ways to check and improve their own technique between classes. Particular interests include safe, sustainable practice, helping older students and those with health conditions to train well, and the traditional supplementary training of Okinawan karate.

*Goju-Ryu Karate Sanchin Masterclass* brings together the methods used in Dr Stivers's dojo: the frame-by-frame teaching of the kata, the testing map, the fault catalogue and the strength progressions.
