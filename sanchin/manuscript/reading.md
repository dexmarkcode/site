# Further Reading

The following books are a good starting point for readers who want to go further. Details of editions change; search by author and title.

**History and the Goju-Ryu system**

- Morio Higaonna, *Traditional Karatedo: Okinawa Goju Ryu* (several volumes). A detailed technical series by a senior teacher of the style, covering fundamentals, kata and supplementary training.
- Morio Higaonna, *The History of Karate: Okinawan Goju-Ryu*. A history of the style and its teachers from within the tradition.
- Patrick McCarthy, *Bible of Karate: Bubishi*. An annotated translation of the Bubishi, the text from which the name Goju-Ryu was taken.
- Mark Bishop, *Okinawan Karate: Teachers, Styles and Secret Techniques*. A survey of Okinawan styles and their teachers, including Goju-Ryu and Uechi-Ryu.

**Sanchin**

- Kris Wilder, *The Way of Sanchin Kata: The Application of Power*. A book-length study of Sanchin from a Goju-Ryu perspective.

**Applications**

- Iain Abernethy, *Bunkai-Jutsu: The Practical Application of Karate Kata*. A widely read introduction to the principles of kata application.

**Breathing, posture and strength**

For the physiology of breathing, posture and strength training, any recent introductory textbook of exercise physiology or strength and conditioning will add depth to Chapters 7, 8 and 22. Readers with health conditions should seek guidance from their own doctor or physiotherapist rather than relying on general texts.

**A note on sources**

Much of what is known about the early history of Sanchin comes from oral tradition within the karate community, and accounts differ. Where this book describes historical events, it gives the account most widely accepted and says where the record is uncertain. Readers interested in the history are encouraged to compare several sources.
