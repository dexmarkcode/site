**Important notice.** Karate is a contact discipline and the training described in this book, including Sanchin testing, conditioning exercises, the use of traditional weighted implements, joint locks, throws and partner drills, carries a real risk of injury. This book is a companion to qualified instruction, not a replacement for it. Consult a doctor before beginning any demanding programme of exercise, particularly if you have high blood pressure, a heart condition, a hernia, joint problems, are pregnant or are recovering from injury. Breath-holding and strong abdominal tension raise blood pressure and must be approached gradually. Practise joint locks, chokes and throws only under supervision, with a cooperative partner, on suitable matting, and release immediately when your partner taps. The author and publisher accept no liability for any injury, loss or damage arising from the use or misuse of the information in this book.

Kata sequences differ in detail between the Okinawan and Japanese branches of Goju-Ryu, and between associations within each branch. Where this book describes a sequence, it follows a widely practised Okinawan form. If your own teacher's version differs, your teacher's version is the one to practise.
