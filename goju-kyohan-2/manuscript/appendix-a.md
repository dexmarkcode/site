# Appendix A: Kata Reference Cards

These cards summarise each kata in movement groups, for quick reference before training. They follow the descriptions in the main chapters. Sequences differ between lineages; where your teacher's version differs, correct the card in pencil and follow your teacher.

## Sanchin

**Character:** The root of the system. Structure, breath, tension and concentration.\
**Stance:** Sanchin dachi throughout.\
**Breath:** Ibuki, slow and continuous.\
**Groups:** (1) Opening: step forward into sanchin kamae. (2) Three steps forward with slow punch and chudan uke. (3) The turns, with punch and chudan uke in the new direction. (4) Closing techniques: double nukite, pull, double palm push, mawashi uke.\
**Watch for:** Rising shoulders, puffed chest, held breath, locked knees, collapse on the turns.\
**See:** Chapter 4.

## Tensho

**Character:** The soft hand. Muchimi, wrist strength, circular movement.\
**Stance:** Sanchin dachi.\
**Breath:** Nogare, or soft ibuki, leading each hand movement.\
**Groups:** (1) Opening. (2) Single-hand cycle, right then left: kake uke, shotei, gedan shotei, tensho uke, koken. (3) Double-hand cycle. (4) Alternating hands and steps. (5) Mawashi uke and close.\
**Watch for:** Floppy wrists, tense fingers, rushing, hands drifting wide.\
**See:** Chapter 5.

## Saifa

**Character:** Escaping from grips; short, circular, explosive.\
**Stances:** Musubi dachi, shiko dachi, seisan or zenkutsu dachi, neko ashi dachi.\
**Groups:** (1) The three tearing releases to the diagonals. (2) Kick, press and back-fist, front and rear. (3) Grip and drop into shiko dachi with hammer-fist, both sides. (4) Double-hand techniques. (5) Double strike in shiko dachi and mawashi uke.\
**Key application:** Release from a wrist grip by turning the whole body against the thumb.\
**See:** Chapter 7.

## Seiyunchin

**Character:** Control, pull and fight. No kicks. Long, deep shiko dachi.\
**Groups:** (1) The three openings to the diagonals: rising wedge, low block, low thrust, pull. (2) Elbow into palm and back-fist, both sides. (3) Forward hook and pull. (4) The lifting movement. (5) Low strikes and mawashi uke.\
**Key application:** Release from a two-handed grip by the drop and the wedge.\
**See:** Chapter 8.

## Shisochin

**Character:** Open hands, shoulder power, four directions.\
**Groups:** (1) Three slow nukite steps with the hooking return. (2) Short palm strikes, single and double. (3) The turns to four directions with kake uke, grip and elbow. (4) Kick and double palm push. (5) Mawashi uke.\
**Key application:** Grip, turn and forearm across the elbow.\
**See:** Chapter 9.

## Sanseru

**Character:** Close range, double hand techniques and low joint kicks.\
**Groups:** (1) Three steps with chudan uke and double punch. (2) Turns with grip and kansetsu geri. (3) Double grab and knee strike. (4) Elbows and dropping strikes in shiko dachi. (5) Mawashi uke.\
**Key application:** Joint kick under control of the hands.\
**See:** Chapter 10.

## Sepai

**Character:** Circles, crossing steps and hidden throws.\
**Groups:** (1) Circular deflection, grip and strike to the diagonals. (2) Crossing steps into kosa dachi with back-fist, ura tsuki and hammer-fist. (3) The wheel and the low strike. (4) The rear elbow and turning strike. (5) Mawashi uke.\
**Key application:** The wheel throw; the rear elbow escape.\
**See:** Chapter 11.

## Kururunfa

**Character:** Stillness and sudden force. Neko ashi dachi dominates.\
**Groups:** (1) Slow open-hand control and palm in neko ashi dachi, to the diagonals. (2) Knee, kick and palm strikes. (3) Double palm strikes in shiko dachi, both sides. (4) The turning takedown. (5) Mawashi uke.\
**Key application:** Kick at the moment of commitment; turning takedown by the arm and shoulder.\
**See:** Chapter 12.

## Seisan

**Character:** Fast, compact, the closest range. Short power.\
**Groups:** (1) Steps with block-punch-punch and open-hand techniques. (2) Kick and crossing step with dropping strike. (3) Grab and knee. (4) Turns with low attacks and joint kick. (5) Mawashi uke.\
**Key application:** Close-range combination with the deflecting arm kept in contact.\
**See:** Chapter 13.

## Suparinpei

**Character:** The longest and most complete kata. A summary of the system.\
**Groups:** (1) Three steps with slow double nukite. (2) The four directions: block, grip, palm, kick, elbow, back-fist. (3) The crane and the tiger. (4) The drop and the low attacks. (5) The arm lock and mawashi uke.\
**Key demands:** Endurance, concentration and pacing.\
**See:** Chapter 14.
