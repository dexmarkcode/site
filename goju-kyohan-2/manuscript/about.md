# About the Author

Dr Beverly J. Stivers has trained in Okinawan Goju-Ryu karate for most of an adult lifetime and has taught students of every age and level, from complete beginners to senior black belts. The teaching is known for its clarity, its patience and its insistence that every technique be understood, tested and made to work, rather than simply performed.

Alongside karate, Dr Stivers brings a long-standing interest in how people learn physical skills, and much of the work on the dojo floor focuses on helping intermediate and senior students diagnose and overcome the faults that hold them back. *Goju-Ryu Karate Kyohan, Volume Two* grew out of many years of notes, corrections and questions gathered in training.

Dr Stivers continues to train and to teach, working, in the author's own words, on "the same things as everyone else, just more slowly".
