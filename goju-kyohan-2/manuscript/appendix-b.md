# Appendix B: Training Plans

The following plans are starting points. Adjust them to your schedule, your level and the advice of your teacher. Each assumes the session structure described in Chapter 15.

## Plan one: twelve weeks to a new kata

Use this plan when you begin learning a new classical kata.

| Weeks | Focus | Main work |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Sequence | Learn the movement groups one at a time in high stances. Name each group. Sketch the floor pattern. |
| 4–6 | Shape | Lower the stances to full depth. Clean up hand positions and directions. Check against the drawings. Ask for corrections. |
| 7–9 | Power | Add breath, timing and rhythm. Perform once a week slowly with tension. Isolate the weakest group. |
| 10–12 | Meaning | Learn one application per group. Test each at levels 1 to 3 of the resistance ladder. |

: **Table B.1** Twelve weeks to a new kata.

## Plan two: the Sanchin repair month

Use this plan when testing shows persistent faults in your Sanchin.

| Week | Daily (10 minutes) | Three times a week (with a partner) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sanchin kamae hold, 3 × 1 minute; wall push drill | Level-one testing (placing) |
| 2 | Hold 3 × 2 minutes; correction drill for your worst fault | Level-two testing (pressing) |
| 3 | Hold 3 × 2 minutes; Sanchin once at reduced intensity | The tested turn drill, 10 each way |
| 4 | Hold 3 × 3 minutes; full Sanchin twice | Full testing at your usual level; compare with week 1 |

: **Table B.2** A four-week Sanchin repair plan.

## Plan three: the weekly rotation

For students who know most or all of the classical kata. See Table 15.2 in Chapter 15.

## Plan four: grading preparation, eight weeks

| Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Perform each grading kata group by group. Record yourself. List three faults per kata. |
| 3–4 | Work on the faults. Slow performances with tension twice a week. Sanchin tested at every session. |
| 5–6 | Full-speed performances with attention to rhythm and breath. Prepare and test applications for each grading kata. |
| 7 | Mock grading in front of your teacher or seniors. Act on their feedback. |
| 8 | Reduce volume. Keep quality high. Rest well in the final days. |

: **Table B.3** Eight weeks of grading preparation.

## Plan five: the older student's week

For students adapting their training to age or injury. Raise stances, lower kicks and reduce tension as described in Chapter 15.

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| 1 | Junbi undo; Sanchin at moderate tension; Tensho; one kata slowly |
| 2 | Rest or a walk |
| 3 | Junbi undo; Tensho twice; kakie; light chi ishi |
| 4 | Rest |
| 5 | Junbi undo; Sanchin; two kata from the rotation; applications at level 1–2 |
| 6–7 | Rest, or gentle stance holds and breathing |

: **Table B.4** A balanced week for the older student.
