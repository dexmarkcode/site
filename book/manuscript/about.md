**Marcus J. Thornton, Ph.D.**, is an electrical engineer and educator who has spent many years teaching circuit theory and electronics to students at every level, from first-year beginners to final-year project students.

His teaching is built on a simple belief: that circuit analysis is not a talent some people are born with, but a set of clear ideas and reliable methods that anyone can learn when they are explained properly and practised patiently. *Circuit Analysis Made Simple* distils that approach into a single volume, combining careful explanation, real-world examples and more than a hundred step-by-step worked solutions.

When he is not writing or teaching, he can usually be found at a workbench with a soldering iron, a multimeter and a circuit that is not yet doing what it should – which, he insists, is the best way there is to learn.
