# About the Author

**Marcus J. Thornton, Ph.D.**, is an engineer and educator whose work centres on electrified powertrains: batteries and their management, electric machines, power electronics and the integration of all three into vehicles that people can rely on. Dr Thornton's teaching and mentoring have reached engineering students at every level and graduate engineers making the move from combustion to electric propulsion.

Among students and colleagues, Dr Thornton is known for one stubborn habit: refusing to accept an explanation until it can be written down with numbers and units. This book is the result of that habit, applied to the whole electric vehicle.
