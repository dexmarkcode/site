# Further Reading

The following books and standards are good next steps for readers who want to go deeper into the topics of each part. Standards are revised regularly; always consult the current edition.

## General electric and hybrid vehicle engineering

- Ehsani, M., Gao, Y., Longo, S. and Ebrahimi, K. *Modern Electric, Hybrid Electric, and Fuel Cell Vehicles*. CRC Press.
- Larminie, J. and Lowry, J. *Electric Vehicle Technology Explained*. Wiley.
- Gillespie, T. D. *Fundamentals of Vehicle Dynamics*. SAE International.

## Batteries and battery management

- Plett, G. L. *Battery Management Systems, Volume I: Battery Modeling*. Artech House.
- Plett, G. L. *Battery Management Systems, Volume II: Equivalent-Circuit Methods*. Artech House.
- Warner, J. T. *The Handbook of Lithium-Ion Battery Pack Design*. Elsevier.

## Electric machines and drives

- Hendershot, J. R. and Miller, T. J. E. *Design of Brushless Permanent-Magnet Machines*. Motor Design Books.
- Krause, P. C., Wasynczuk, O., Sudhoff, S. D. and Pekarek, S. *Analysis of Electric Machinery and Drive Systems*. Wiley–IEEE Press.

## Power electronics and semiconductors

- Mohan, N., Undeland, T. M. and Robbins, W. P. *Power Electronics: Converters, Applications, and Design*. Wiley.
- Erickson, R. W. and Maksimović, D. *Fundamentals of Power Electronics*. Springer.
- Holmes, D. G. and Lipo, T. A. *Pulse Width Modulation for Power Converters: Principles and Practice*. Wiley–IEEE Press.
- Baliga, B. J. *Fundamentals of Power Semiconductor Devices*. Springer.

## Key standards and regulations referred to in this book

- UN Regulation No. 100: electrical safety of battery electric vehicles (including Part II on the rechargeable energy storage system).
- UN Regulations No. 10 (EMC), 94, 95 and 137 (crash protection), 155 (cyber security) and 156 (software updates).
- ISO 6469: electrically propelled road vehicles — safety specifications.
- ISO 26262: road vehicles — functional safety.
- ISO 21448: road vehicles — safety of the intended functionality.
- ISO/SAE 21434: road vehicles — cybersecurity engineering.
- ISO 17840: road vehicles — information for first and second responders (rescue sheets).
- IEC 61851: electric vehicle conductive charging system.
- IEC 62196: plugs, socket-outlets, vehicle connectors and vehicle inlets.
- ISO 15118: vehicle-to-grid communication interface.
- DIN SPEC 70121: digital communication for DC charging.
- SAE J1772, SAE J3400 and SAE J2954: North American charging connector and wireless charging standards.
- IEC 61000-3-2 and 61000-3-12: limits for harmonic currents.
- CISPR 12 and CISPR 25: radio disturbance limits for vehicles and components.
- UN Manual of Tests and Criteria, Section 38.3: transport testing of lithium batteries.
- GB 38031: safety requirements for traction batteries (China).
- Open Charge Point Protocol (OCPP), published by the Open Charge Alliance.
