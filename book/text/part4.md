# The Z-Transform {.part}

::: partintro
The Z-transform does for discrete-time systems what the Laplace transform does for continuous ones. Difference equations become algebra, convolution becomes multiplication, and stability becomes a question of where the poles sit relative to the unit circle.

Chapter 10 builds the transform and its region of convergence. Chapter 11 inverts it and solves difference equations with initial conditions. Chapter 12 puts everything to work on digital filters: their frequency responses, their stability, their design from analogue prototypes, and their realisation in hardware and software.

You will notice that almost every idea in this part has an exact parallel in Part III. Use that. When you are stuck on a Z-transform problem, ask what the Laplace version would be, and translate.
:::
