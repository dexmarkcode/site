# Fourier Analysis {.part}

::: partintro
Fourier's great idea was that complicated signals can be built from simple sinusoids. For LTI systems this changes everything: each sinusoid passes through on its own, merely scaled and delayed, so the hard problem of finding an output becomes a list of easy ones.

Chapter 4 treats periodic signals with the Fourier series. Chapter 5 lets the period grow without limit and arrives at the Fourier transform. Chapter 6 brings the ideas into discrete time with the DTFT and the DFT, and confronts the question every digital engineer must answer: how fast do I need to sample?

Throughout this part, keep asking two questions of every result. What does it look like in time? What does it look like in frequency? Fluency in moving between the two is the real goal.
:::
