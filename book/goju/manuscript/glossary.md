# Glossary

Bubishi
:   A Chinese manual of fighting principles, techniques and medicine that circulated in Okinawa; source of the phrase from which Goju-Ryu takes its name.

Chi-ishi
:   "Strength stone"; a weight on the end of a wooden handle used to strengthen the grip, wrists and shoulders.

Dachi
:   Stance.

Gekisai Dai Ichi, Gekisai Dai Ni
:   Introductory kata created by Miyagi Chōjun around 1940.

Go
:   Hard; the hard phase of the hard–soft cycle.

Hachiji dachi
:   Natural stance with the feet hip-width apart and the toes turned out.

Heiko dachi
:   Natural stance with the feet parallel and hip-width apart.

Hikite
:   The pulling hand drawn back to the side of the body.

Hojo undō
:   Supplementary exercise; the strength-training tradition of Goju-Ryu.

Ibuki
:   The hard, audible breath of Sanchin.

Ishi-sashi
:   A stone or iron weight shaped like a padlock.

Jari-bako
:   A box of gravel, sand or beans used to condition the fingers.

Ju
:   Soft; the soft phase of the hard–soft cycle.

Junbi undō
:   Preparatory exercises performed at the start of training.

Kakie
:   Pushing-hands exercise in which partners keep their wrists in contact.

Kake uke
:   Hooking block made with the bent wrist.

Kamae
:   Guard or ready posture.

Kata
:   A prescribed sequence of movements practised alone.

Ki
:   Traditional term for vital energy; used in this book as shorthand for a set of trainable internal skills.

Kiai
:   A sharp exhalation with a sound at the moment of a decisive technique.

Kongōken
:   A large, heavy oval iron ring used for strength training.

Kōken
:   The top of the bent wrist, used for blocking and striking.

Kote kitae
:   Forearm conditioning, usually with a partner.

Kuzushi
:   Off-balancing an opponent.

Makiwara
:   A flexible striking post with a padded top.

Mawashi uke
:   The circular block of both hands, characteristic of Goju-Ryu.

Mokuso
:   Quiet sitting with closed or lowered eyes, at the start or end of class.

Musubi dachi
:   Attention stance with heels together and toes apart.

Naha-te
:   The karate of Naha, from which Goju-Ryu developed.

Neko ashi dachi
:   Cat stance, with nearly all the weight on the rear foot.

Nigiri-game
:   Gripping jars, held by their rims.

Nogare
:   The soft, controlled breath.

Rei
:   Bow. *Ritsu-rei* is the standing bow; *za-rei* the kneeling bow.

Sanchin
:   "Three battles"; the root kata of Goju-Ryu, and its stance.

Seiza
:   Formal kneeling posture.

Shiko dachi
:   Wide, low stance with the toes turned out.

Shime
:   Testing Sanchin by pressing, pushing and striking.

Shōtei
:   The heel of the palm.

Tan
:   A stone or weighted barbell.

Tanden
:   The centre of the body in the lower abdomen (Chinese *dantian*).

Tensho
:   "Rotating palms"; the soft breathing kata created by Miyagi Chōjun.

Tetsu-geta
:   Iron sandals for leg strengthening.

Uke
:   Block or receiving technique; also the partner who receives a technique.

Yōi
:   Ready.

Zanshin
:   Remaining mind; continued alert awareness after a technique.

Zenkutsu dachi
:   Forward stance.
