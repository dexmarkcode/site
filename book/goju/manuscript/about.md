# About the Author

Dr Beverly J. Stivers has practised and taught Goju-Ryu karate for more than three decades. Her teaching concentrates on the inner training of the style: Sanchin and Tensho, breath control, rooting, traditional hojo undō and the patient, progressive conditioning that keeps students training safely into later life.

Her approach combines the methods of traditional Okinawan practice with a careful reading of modern exercise science and anatomy. She is known for her problem-solving style of instruction, which treats every fault as a puzzle with a cause and a cure, and for her work with older students, many of whom began training in their sixties and seventies.

She teaches regular classes and seminars, and continues to practise Sanchin every morning.

*Goju-Ryu Karate Kyohan* is the textbook she wished she had owned as a young student.
