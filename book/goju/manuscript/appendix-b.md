## Reference: stances at a glance

| Stance | Width | Length | Weight | Toes | Knees |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Musubi dachi | Heels together | None | 50/50 | Out ~45° | Soft |
| Heiko dachi | Hip-width | None | 50/50 | Forward | Soft |
| Hachiji dachi | Hip-width | None | 50/50 | Out | Soft |
| Sanchin dachi | Outside of shoulders | Front heel to back toes | 50/50 | In | Drawn in over feet |
| Shiko dachi | ~2 shoulder-widths | None | 50/50 | Out ~45° | Pressed out over toes |
| Zenkutsu dachi | Hip- to shoulder-width | ~1.5 shoulder-widths | 60/40 | Front forward; back slightly out | Front over foot; back firm |
| Neko ashi dachi | About a fist | About a foot-length | 10/90 | Front on ball | Back knee over foot |

## Reference: breathing at a glance

| Breath | In | Out | Sound | Tension | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Natural | Nose, low | Nose | Silent | None | Default |
| Ibuki | Nose, 1–2 s | Mouth, narrowed throat, long | Audible | High, held | Sanchin |
| Tensho breath | Nose, slow | Mouth or nose, smooth | Soft | Rising and falling | Tensho |
| Nogare | Nose, quiet | Controlled release | Soft or silent | Moderate | Recovery; safe Sanchin |
| Kiai | — | Sharp burst from abdomen | Short, deep | Peak | Decisive technique |
