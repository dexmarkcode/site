**A note on the kata.** Gōjū-ryū is taught by many associations, and the fine detail of each kata (the count, the direction of a turn, the hand that leads) varies between them. The sequences in this book follow one consistent teaching version in the Okinawan line of Miyagi Chōjun and point out the main variations. Where your own teacher's version differs, your teacher's version is the one to grade with.

Japanese terms are given with macrons (Gōjū-ryū, Chōjun) where it helps pronunciation; in headings and count sheets they are sometimes omitted, as they are in most dojo.

British English spelling is used throughout.
