# Appendix C: The Kata at a Glance

This appendix gives a one-page summary of each kata, for quick revision before a class or a grading. The details are taken from the relevant chapter, which you should consult for the full sequence, the diagrams and the bunkai. Grades and durations are typical rather than fixed; always check your own association's syllabus.

## Gekisai Dai Ichi

Table: **Gekisai Dai Ichi at a glance.**

| | |
|:----|:----------|
| Meaning | Attack and destroy, no. 1 |
| Family | Fukyūgata (promotional) |
| Origin | Miyagi Chōjun, 1940 |
| Typical grade | 1st–2nd kyū |
| Counts in this book | 26 (plus rei and yōi) |
| Embusen | T shape |
| Stances used | heiko dachi, musubi dachi, sanchin dachi, shiko dachi, zenkutsu dachi |
| Kiai | count 14 |
| Typical duration | 50–70 s |
| Rhythm | Brisk, even; natural breathing |
| Full treatment | Chapter 4 |

**Three things to remember:**

1. Look before the first step
2. Land into the elbow strike
3. Hips drive the turn into shiko dachi
