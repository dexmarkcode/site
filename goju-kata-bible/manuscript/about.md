# About the Author

Dr Beverly J. Stivers has practised and taught Gōjū-ryū karate for many years, with a particular interest in the kaishu kata, their applications and the way they are passed from teacher to student. Alongside training, Dr Stivers has studied the history of Okinawan karate and the ways in which physical skills are learned and retained.

Dr Stivers teaches adults and young people, sits on grading panels and runs workshops on kata, bunkai and grading preparation, with a teaching style known for patience, close attention to stance and breath, and an insistence that every movement in a kata should mean something.

This book grew out of the notes, diagrams and checklists prepared for students over many years, and out of their repeated request for a single volume that set out every kata clearly, from the first Gekisai to Suparinpei.

Dr Stivers still practises Sanchin every day.
