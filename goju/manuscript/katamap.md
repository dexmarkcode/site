# Appendix B: Kata Application Map

This map links the close-range techniques in this book to the Goju-Ryu kata in which I find them. It is not an official list, and other teachers will read some movements differently. Use it as a starting point for your own bunkai study, and test every interpretation with the four tests from Chapter 1: pressure, context, consistency and follow-through.

| Kata | Close-range themes | Where to look | Chapters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sanchin | Structure, breathing, elbows down, crescent step | The whole kata; shime testing | 2, 3 |
| Tensho | Sensitivity, circling hands, soft redirection | The open-hand circles; mawashi uke | 4, 5 |
| Gekisai Dai Ichi | Angles, cover entries, short strikes | Angled steps; rising blocks as covers | 3, 6 |
| Gekisai Dai Ni | Open-hand traps, angled entries | Open-hand techniques; turning steps | 3, 5 |
| Saifa | Wrist-grab escape, back fist, hammer fist, outer reap | The opening; step-and-strike sequences | 5, 6, 11, 15 |
| Seiyunchin | Pulls with strikes, rising elbow, shoulder wheel | Low-stance pulling; high–low arm positions | 6, 7, 11 |
| Shisochin | Pressing hands, arm-bar takedown, turning controls | The opening pressing movements; turns | 10, 11, 13 |
| Sanseru | Knees, low kicks, body strikes, pulls | Kicks with pulling hands | 8 |
| Seipai | Clinch breaks, elbows, turning throws, wrist bend | Rising and dropping arms; turns | 7, 9, 11, 14 |
| Kururunfa | Holding and striking, clinch, joint controls, dropping | Circling and pressing; deep drops | 9, 12, 13 |
| Seisan | Stamps, outer reap, short strikes | Heavy steps; strike-and-step sequences | 8, 11 |
| Suparinpei | Stamps, turns, level changes, combinations | Stance changes; turning sequences | 8, 11, 12 |

## Common movements and their close-range readings

| Movement | Usual name | Close-range reading | Chapter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hand drawn back to the hip | Hikite | A hand that has caught an arm and is pulling | 5 |
| Hooking block | Kake uke | Hook over an arm and pull into control | 5 |
| Circular double-hand block | Mawashi uke / tora guchi | Pass, pin and double push or strike | 5 |
| Rising block | Age uke | Cover entry, elbow lift, forearm strike | 3, 5 |
| Low sweeping block | Gedan barai | Arm pass, hammer fist to the thigh, sweep | 5, 6 |
| Heavy forward step | Stance change | Stamp onto foot or knee | 8 |
| Turn of 90 or 180 degrees | Facing a new attacker | Turning throw while holding | 10, 11 |
| Low straddle stance with pull | Shiko dachi | Drop under the centre for a throw | 11 |
| Hand drawn to chest, other arm pressing | Block or press | Pin a grabbing hand; inward wrist bend | 14 |
| Supported strike, one hand behind the other | Morote zuki or elbow | Supported forward elbow at chest range | 7 |
