# About the Author

Dr Beverly J. Stivers has studied and taught karate for more than three decades, with a particular focus on Goju-Ryu and the close-range applications of its kata. Dr Stivers' teaching is known for its plain language, its problem-solving approach and its insistence on safe, progressive pressure-testing of every technique.

Alongside traditional training, Dr Stivers has spent many years cross-training with judoka, boxers, wrestlers and professionals whose work brings them into contact with aggression, adapting what proved effective to the framework of the Goju-Ryu kata. The author has taught classes and seminars for karateka of all styles and ages, as well as practical self-protection courses for people with no martial arts background.

Dr Stivers believes that the close range is where most people need help and receive least, and that a good martial art should make its students calmer, kinder and much harder to hurt.

*Goju-Ryu Karate Close-Combat Masterclass* gathers the methods developed and tested with students in the author's own classes over many years.
