# Important Safety and Legal Notice

Martial arts training involves physical contact, falls and the risk of injury. The techniques described in this book include strikes, throws, chokes and joint controls that can cause serious harm, including permanent injury, if they are practised carelessly or applied with excessive force.

This book is for informational and educational purposes only. It is not a substitute for instruction from a qualified, insured martial arts teacher, and it is not medical advice. Consult your doctor before beginning any new training programme, particularly if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, joint problems, a history of injury, or are pregnant. Never practise throws or falls without suitable mats and supervision, and never practise joint controls, neck restraints or strikes to vulnerable areas at full force on a training partner.

Nothing in this book is legal advice. The law on self-defence varies between countries and, within the United Kingdom, between jurisdictions. In general you may use only such force as is reasonable in the circumstances as you honestly believe them to be. The use of force can have serious legal consequences. If you are in any doubt about your legal position, seek advice from a qualified solicitor.

Readers are responsible for their own decisions and actions. The author and publisher disclaim any liability for injury, loss or damage arising directly or indirectly from the use or misuse of the information in this book.

Train safely, train with people you trust, and look after each other.
